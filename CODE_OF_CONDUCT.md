# Code of Conduct

LR-Agent is an open research and engineering project.

Be respectful, specific and evidence-oriented.

## Expected behavior

- Critique ideas and implementations, not people.
- Share reproducible evidence when possible.
- Treat negative results and counterexamples as valuable.
- Give credit for prior work and corrections.
- Keep discussions focused on the technical/research question.
- Do not expose private data, credentials or confidential repository content.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, deliberate doxxing, spam, fabricated benchmark results, or knowingly misleading claims are not acceptable.

## Research disagreement

Disagreement about novelty, metrics or experimental design is welcome.

When a claim is contested, prefer:

- links to prior work;
- minimal reproductions;
- benchmark evidence;
- concrete alternative interpretations.

The goal is to make the project more correct, not to “win” an argument.

## Reporting

For normal project conduct concerns, open a repository issue only when doing so is safe and appropriate. Security-sensitive matters should follow [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md).
